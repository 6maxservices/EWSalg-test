# NDA template field checklist

This is an empty field checklist for completion by qualified counsel. It is not
contract language or legal advice.

- [ ] Parties and legal names
- [ ] Effective date
- [ ] Definition of confidential information
- [ ] Permitted purpose and recipients
- [ ] Exclusions
- [ ] Security and incident duties
- [ ] Return/deletion process
- [ ] Term and survival
- [ ] Governing law and venue
- [ ] Signatures and authority
- [ ] Counsel review completed
